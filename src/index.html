Just an ordinary human, happy with small things.

I’m Raihan Fathoni, a student at Gunadarma University and a beginner developer currently diving into AI development, backend engineering and data analysis.

My curiosity refuses to stay in one lane. I make projects about mathematics, science and music, contribute to open source, and occasionally disappear into a spreadsheet for six hours. One of my favourite tools is still Excel — not just for data, but for theory crafting game mechanics.

I like software that feels small, deliberate and quietly useful. The kind you don’t notice until it saves someone an afternoon.
